Simple steps, real impact

Good food deserves a good destination.

We make it easier for donors and community organisations to coordinate food donations responsibly.

🍲

1. Share what you have

Restaurants, shops, event organisers and households can tell us about safe, surplus food available to donate.

🤝

2. Find a community partner

FoodBridge helps connect donors with local trusts and community groups that can coordinate collection.

💚

3. Pass kindness on

Partners arrange collection and distribution, following appropriate food-safety and handling practices.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Who can donate food?

Food businesses, event organisers, community groups and households may express interest. Donations must be suitable for safe handling and consumption.

Can a trust or NGO partner with FoodBridge?

Yes. Community organisations can register their interest so the project team can discuss needs, location, collection and distribution arrangements.

Does this website currently arrange pickups?

Not yet. This is a front-end website prototype. A real service needs a verified partner network, food-safety procedures, reliable contact channels and a working request-management system.
